Claude PP — comments

Valuation key (same units: Minor = 1, Major = 2, Milestone = 5). Items are rated by effect, not by label. The sample patron is the Fiend.

  • +1 Spell Known = 0.5. Warlock is bottlenecked by 2 slots, so extra spells add flexibility, not output.

  • +1 Cantrip = 0.5.

  • Invocation = 1.5 on average. The range is wide: ribbons like Eyes of the Rune Keeper ≈ 0.5, Thirsting Blade ≈ 3 for a blade build.

  • Feat / ASI = 2.

  • Spell circle: 2nd = 1.5, 3rd = 3, 4th = 2, 5th = 3. The 3rd and 5th circles are 5e breakpoints (Fireball / Hold Monster tier).

  • Patron spells (2 fixed spells that don't count against Spells Known) = 1 per batch.

  • Level 1 — 4. Eldritch Blast (1) + 2 Invocations (1.5 each). Same as yours, but EB itself is counted.

  • Level 2 — 7. Pact Magic unlock: 2 short-rest slots (2) + 2 Cantrips (1) + 3 Spells Known (1.5) + patron package (2.5): Fiendish Magic (EB d10 + ignore fire resistance) + patron spells + Dark Vitality. Yours is 3 because it doesn't count the patron. For Elara the Contract replaces the patron, but for comparing her to other characters the patron has to be counted.

  • Level 3 — 6. Eldritch Blade (2: the gish enabler, Pact mod to attacks, conjured weapon) + 3rd Invocation (1.5) + 2nd circle (1.5) + patron spells (1).

  • Level 4 — 3. ASI (2) + Cantrip (0.5) + Spell Known (0.5). Matches yours.

  • Level 5 — 8.5. Biggest jump in 5e. Empowered Blast (3: doubles at-will damage, effectively Extra Attack for a blaster) + Invocation (1.5) + 3rd circle (3) + patron spells (1). For a blade build, swap the weights: Empowered Blast ≈ 1, but this is when Thirsting Blade becomes available (≈ 3).

  • Level 6 — 3. Fiend gets two features: Dark Bargain (2: +d10 to a failed check/save, repeatable) + Accursed Flames (0.5), plus Spell Known (0.5).

  • Level 7 — 4.5. Invocation (1.5) + 4th circle (2) + patron spells (1).

  • Level 8 — 2.5. ASI (2) + Spell Known (0.5). The quietest level.

  • Level 9 — 5.5. Invocation (1.5) + 5th circle (3) + patron spells (1).

  • Level 10 — 2.5. Fiendish Resilience (1.5: one chosen resistance per rest) + Cantrip (0.5) + Spell Known (0.5).

  • Level 11 — 8. Elder Arcanum (3: a 6th-level spell 1/long rest, or Occult Warrior's +1d8 per blade hit) + 3 beams (2) + 3rd slot (3: +50% spell budget). The value at 11 comes more from the slot and the beams than from the Arcanum; 5 PP for an Arcanum is high.

Cumulative, Claude vs yours (as of your current Total column): L3 17 / 11 · L5 28.5 / 19 · L7 36 / 26 · L8 38.5 / 29 · L11 54.5 / 44.

Takeaways:

  • In 5e, value is front-loaded (levels 1–3) and spikes on odd levels (new spell circle + patron spells). Even levels are quiet.
  • For Elara: see the benchmark and comparison tables below. Per the benchmark, one warlock level is worth 2–9.5 PP depending on the level (≈ 5 on average for levels 7–11, ≈ 3 for levels 12–20), so 7 PP (2 Major + 3 Minor) per Contract level is roughly one to one and a half warlock levels.
  • Patron items (≈ 2.5 at L2, ≈ 1 on odd levels, ≈ 2–2.5 at L6/L10) are the part the Contract replaces. Her talent trees should cover that value, not just the class columns.

Revised after a full read of the Alternate Warlock text. What changed and why:

  • Level 1 picks. Armor of Shadows requires 3rd level, so it moves to level 3. Level 1 can only take invocations without prerequisites, which are all utility.
  • Improved Pact Weapon removed. The document has no such invocation. Bonding a magic weapon and using the Pact modifier are part of the base Eldritch Blade.
  • Eldritch Blade 3 → 2. At level 3 it adds about 1.5 damage over Eldritch Blast. Its real value arrives with Thirsting Blade and the blade Arcana, which are priced separately.
  • Thirsting Blade 3.5 → 4, beams 1 → 1.5 (levels 5 and 11). Thirsting Blade gives a bonus-action blade attack after Eldritch Blast or any Warlock spell, so the beams and the blade add together. At-will damage with every attack hitting: 30 at level 5, 48 at level 11.
  • Tomb of Frost 2 → 3. A reaction that absorbs 10 × level damage and makes her untargetable until her turn. Free once per short rest, then for a slot.
  • Dark Bargain 2 → 2.5, Accursed Flames 1 → 0.5. Dark Bargain has no use limit. Accursed Flames is situational.
  • Favored Servant replaces Arcane Secrets. It casts a patron spell at full slot level (a 5th-level Fireball), while Arcane Secrets casts at the spell's lowest level.
  • 3rd slot 3 → 4, 4th slot 2 → 2.5. Slots also pay for extra uses of Tomb of Frost, Eldritch Wind and Awakened Blade.
  • Otherworldly Step 3.5 → 2.5. Three blade attacks (49.5) barely beat Eldritch Blast + bonus blade attack (48). The gain is the freed bonus action and a 10 ft teleport per attack.
  • Eldritch Wind 3.5 → 3. 6d10 replaces weapon damage, so it's weaker than a normal round against one target. It needs 3+ targets to pay off.

Optimized ceiling (no multiclassing): about +3 PP over this benchmark at level 7 and +5 to +6 at level 20.

  • Invocation swaps. One invocation can be replaced per level, so both level 1 utility picks become combat picks: Otherworldly Vigor, Enspelled Blade with Repelling Blast or Lance of Lethargy, more Favored Servants.
  • Feats instead of ASIs.
  • Spirit Shroud. In a 5th-level slot it adds 2d8 to every hit within 10 ft, beams included: 84 per round at level 11 and about 103 at level 17, before accuracy.
  • Reach weapon. The beams have disadvantage with an enemy within 5 ft until Awakened Blade (level 17). A glaive at 10 ft avoids that.
